publisher: Lothlorien

summary: A near real-time wargame based on a famous WW2 counter-offensive.
---

The game loaded despite the felt header being in a deteriorated state being mounted on the cassette backplate rather than the normal metal spring. The loading screen is the main in-game interface with a window on a much larger map.

The game controls are simple, QAOP and Space to fire. And you control a selection box from which you can issue orders to your side, and see the status of the opposing army.
